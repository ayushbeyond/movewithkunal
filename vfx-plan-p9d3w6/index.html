Kick shockwave

Cold open, 0.9–2.0s (replaces the kick shot)

Must do

Makes the first second impossible to scroll past, and it's real footage plus one effect, so his face stays his.

Method: First + last frame

  1. Firefly Image → Edit (Generative fill) on 15_kick_PEAK with the image prompt to make the END frame.
  2. Generate video: First frame = 15_kick_PEAK, Last frame = your edited image, 9:16, 1080p.
  3. Use the first 1.2s only; speed-ramp 100% → 30% at impact.
Image edit prompt (makes the last frame)

Add a circular shockwave of fine grey dust bursting outward from the kicking foot at head height, small concrete chips and dust lifting off the paved floor around the standing foot, slight air distortion around the foot, same overcast daylight, photorealistic. Keep the man, his face, white t-shirt, navy track pants, the wall and the KOF banner exactly the same.

Video prompt

Slow motion, 120fps look. A martial artist holds a high side kick in an outdoor training yard. At the moment of impact a ring of dust and air distortion bursts outward from his foot and dust lifts off the floor. Tiny camera push-in with a short shake on impact. Overcast natural daylight, photorealistic sports commercial. His body and face stay steady and unchanged.

SFX: Deep boom + dust burst

'No magic' — energy that shatters

On “iske peeche koi magic nahi” (~5.0–6.5s)

Must do

Shows the line literally: a fake superpower appears, then breaks into dust. It fits the message and is a strong loop moment.

Method: Same image as first AND last frame

  1. Generate video: First = 02_wall-shadowbox_MAGIC, Last = the SAME image. That makes it cut in and out of the real shot with no jump.
  2. Pick the take where the face drifts least; use about 1.5s.
Video prompt

Static camera. A man in a boxing stance against a plain white wall. Swirling electric-blue energy and glowing particles form around both of his fists and forearms, glowing brighter for a moment, then crack like glass and disintegrate into grey dust that blows away to the right, leaving his normal fists. He holds the same stance the whole time. Natural daylight, photorealistic.

SFX: Electric hum → glass crack → sand whoosh

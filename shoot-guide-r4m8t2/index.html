Shoot guide · @movewithkunal

What to change on the next shoot so reels look sharper, edit faster and can take AI effects. Based on the “consistency” reel; the photos are frames from it.

What to fix from this shoot

White tee on a grey wall

What we sawHe blends into the background in flat light, so the grade has to work hard and it still looks dull.

FixWear a black or KOF-yellow tee, or train in front of the KOF banner or a darker wall.

Captions burned into the export

What we sawThey can't be removed. We had to zoom in about 17% to hide them, which cut off his feet.

FixSend raw clips with no text, plus a separate voiceover. Text gets added in the edit.

Feet cut, no room around him

What we sawKicks and stances run off the bottom edge, and there's no space left for captions or effects.

FixFrame head-to-toe with about 15% space above and below. Keep the action in the middle 60% of the frame.

Same angle every shot

What we sawEverything is eye-level and mid-wide from a locked tripod, so 30 seconds feels like one shot.

FixFor every move, film wide + close-up (hands or feet) + low angle. Low angles make kicks look powerful.

Brightness and colour jump between shots

What we sawThe camera re-exposes between setups, so cuts don't match.

FixOn iPhone, long-press to lock AE/AF before each take, and film everything in one session.

Weak opening shot

What we sawHands on hips while talking: nothing happens in the first second.

FixStart on the most impressive move (kick, staff spin). Film the talking line separately as a close-up.

Long repeated shadowboxing

What we sawAbout 13 seconds of similar shadowboxing. People get bored.

FixPlan 1–2s purposeful clips. Film 3 takes of each and keep the best.

Cluttered background

What we sawBamboo poles, pipes, tyres and chains pull the eye away from him.

FixWalk the frame before filming. Move clutter or pick a cleaner angle.

Camera settings

  • Vertical 9:16, 4K 30fps for normal shots
  • Slo-mo 1080p 120fps for kicks and hits
  • Lock AE/AF (long-press) before every take
  • Wipe the lens; stabilisation on
  • Tripod at waist height, or lower for kicks

Look & light

  • Film 7–9am or 4–6pm: softer, warmer light
  • Sun to the side of him, never behind
  • Black or yellow tee against the light wall
  • Same outfit for the whole reel
  • Clear clutter out of the frame

Sound

  • Record the voiceover separately in a quiet room
  • Lav mic about 15cm below the chin
  • Record 10s of silence (room tone)
  • Real hit sounds on the bag: record them
  • No music while filming

Ready for AI effects

  • Hold the start and end poses still for 2s
  • Leave empty space around him for effects
  • 3s clean plate (empty frame) per setup
  • Face in sharp focus in at least one take
  • Send raw files: no text, no filters

How to send files

Upload original files from the Photos app (AirDrop or Google Drive “original quality”). WhatsApp compresses video, so don't send it that way. Name the folder with the date and the reel topic.
